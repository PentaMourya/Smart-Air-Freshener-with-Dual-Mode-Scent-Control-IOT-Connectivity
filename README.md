## Smart Air Freshener with Dual Mode Scent Control IOT Connectivity

**Project Overview:**
The Smart Air Freshener is an advanced, IoT-enabled environmental control system engineered for precision scent dispersion, redefining the capabilities of conventional air care devices. Moving beyond the limitations of basic timers, this intelligent unit leverages real-time environmental data and embedded machine learning algorithms to maintain ideal olfactory conditions, ensuring a consistently pleasant fragrance experience while maximizing fluid efficiency and minimizing waste. By intelligently adapting to its surroundings rather than operating on a fixed schedule, the system provides a truly responsive and data-driven approach to ambient scenting for modern smart homes and office environments.

**Dual‑Mode Scent Control and Coverage:**
At the heart of the device lies a versatile dual‑mode control system, offering both an Intelligent Mode and a Manual Mode to suit varying user preferences. In Intelligent Mode, the device automatically calibrates its spray frequency based on room occupancy and air quality, utilizing a sophisticated sensor suite that detects Volatile Organic Compounds with a sensitivity of 0.1 ppm and adjusts output accordingly. For users who prefer direct oversight, Manual Mode provides precision timing intervals ranging from 1 minute to 120 minutes, with the capability to deliver burst outputs of 0.5 ml per spray cycle. Optimized for spaces up to 500 square feet, the device achieves a uniform scent concentration of 2 µg/m³ within just 5 minutes of activation, ensuring rapid and even distribution across the target area.

**Fluid Efficiency, Power Management, and Maintenance:**
The Smart Air Freshener distinguishes itself through remarkable fluid and energy efficiency, driven by its smart scheduling algorithms that reduce fragrance fluid consumption by up to 40% when compared to traditional 9‑minute interval sprayers. To guarantee uninterrupted operation, the system features a low‑fluid detection mechanism that alerts users when levels drop below 15%, allowing for timely refills and ensuring 100% uptime of your scent strategy. Power management is equally refined, with an ultra‑low standby consumption of just 0.5W and a battery life that extends up to 6 months under normal usage conditions when powered by three AA batteries, making it both cost‑effective and environmentally considerate.

**Robust IoT Connectivity and Real‑Time Data Monitoring:**
Connectivity forms the backbone of the device’s intelligence, featuring dual‑band 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz Wi‑Fi alongside Bluetooth 5.0 to ensure robust and versatile networking. Advanced retry logic guarantees a 99.9% data transmission success rate, providing unwavering reliability. The system actively sends data to the cloud every 60 seconds, tracking a comprehensive set of usage metrics that include a total sprays counter with an accuracy of ±1 spray, remaining fluid volume measured to within ±2 ml, and ambient humidity and temperature readings with ±0.5°C accuracy. Furthermore, the device supports Over‑The‑Air (OTA) firmware updates, enabling seamless feature upgrades and security patches without any system downtime, ensuring the unit remains future‑proof throughout its lifecycle.

**Smart Home Ecosystem Integration and Technical Specifications:**
Seamless integration with major smart home ecosystems is a cornerstone of the design, as the device is fully compatible with Amazon Alexa, Google Home, and IFTTT. Command execution is exceptionally responsive, with latency from voice trigger to actual spray action clocking in at under 500 milliseconds. Users can also program sophisticated routines based on external metrics—for example, automatically disabling the device when humidity exceeds 60% to prevent scent clogging or degradation. From a technical standpoint, the unit delivers a selectable aerosol output of 0.3 ml per spray on the high setting and 0.1 ml on the low setting, operates at a whisper‑quiet decibel level of less than 30 dB, and features a generous 120 ml refillable tank, all while maintaining the aforementioned sensor accuracy for temperature (±0.5°C) and relative humidity (±5% RH) as detailed in the specifications.

**A Data‑Driven Solution for Smarter Air Care:**
By integrating these advanced metrics and connectivity features, the Smart Air Freshener transcends its role as a simple dispenser to become a fully analytics‑driven environmental tool. Users are empowered to track key performance indicators such as "Scent Usage per Square Foot," enabling data‑backed optimization of operational costs rather than relying on guesswork. The combination of a 40% reduction in fluid waste and the industry‑leading 99.9% connectivity reliability guarantee positions this device as the most efficient air care solution currently available for smart homes and offices. The project is proudly offered under the MIT license, inviting collaboration and innovation in the pursuit of smarter, healthier indoor environments.
